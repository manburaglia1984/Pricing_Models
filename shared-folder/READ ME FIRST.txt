PURCHASE/SALE FACILITY - PRICING MODEL
Shared folder setup
======================================================================

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER

  index.html            The pricing model. Open this.
  pricing-store.json    The data. Created on first use - do not open or
                        edit it by hand.
  pricing-store.lock.json
                        Bookkeeping: who has the model open and who is
                        currently allowed to edit. Ignore it.
  backups\              A timestamped copy of the data every few minutes.
                        This is your safety net.

Use Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari cannot write to a folder and will
open the model read-only, saving to the browser instead.


FIRST TIME - THE PERSON WHO ALREADY HAS THE DEALS GOES FIRST

This matters. Whoever links the folder first puts their data into it.

  1. Make sure this folder is synced to your PC (OneDrive / SharePoint,
     "Sync" or "Add shortcut to OneDrive"). You need a real folder in
     File Explorer, not a browser tab.

  2. Open index.html from the synced folder - double-click it.

  3. On the Deals tab, top card, press "Use a shared folder..." and
     choose THIS folder.

  4. Check the card says "Editing <folder>/pricing-store.json". Your
     deals are now in the folder.

If the model tells you "The shared file is older than what this browser
holds", stop and read it. It means somebody else linked the folder before
you and your deals are the newer ones. Press "Save a backup copy first",
then "Keep mine and save it to the folder". Nothing is overwritten until
you choose.


EVERYONE ELSE - ONCE, ON EACH PC

  1. Open index.html from the synced folder.
  2. Press "Use a shared folder..." and choose this folder.
  3. Set "Acting as" in the top right to your own name. This is what the
     audit log records.

You each do this on your own machine. The choice is remembered, so it is
a one-off - but the browser will ask permission again each time you open
the model. Press "Reconnect" when it does. Until you do, nothing is
being saved to the folder.


DAY TO DAY - ONE PERSON EDITS AT A TIME

Two people may have the model open. Only one can edit.

  - The one who opens first gets the pen and can edit normally.
  - The second sees a yellow "Read-only" bar. They can look at
    everything, run the parity tests, export, and print - but not
    change anything. Their screen follows the editor's saves, so it
    stays current on its own.
  - When you finish, press "Hand the pen over". The other person picks
    it up within about 15 seconds.
  - If you forget, or your PC sleeps, the pen frees itself after 90
    seconds and they can take it.
  - A third person is refused, and told who has it.

Do not try to work around the read-only bar by editing a copy. That is
exactly what creates the mess below.


WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

"OneDrive left a conflict copy in the folder" (red)
  Two saves collided. Nothing is lost, but somebody's work went into a
  file named like pricing-store-DESKTOP-XYZ.json instead of the real
  one. Open both, decide which is right, and use Import JSON to load the
  good one. Then delete the stray file.

"Could not save - the shared file changed since this session last read
it"
  Someone else's copy arrived while you were working. Press "Reload from
  the shared file" to take theirs, or "Force save" to keep yours. Force
  save overwrites theirs - the version you overwrite stays in backups\.

"This session has changes it can no longer save"
  You lost the pen while holding edits. Press "Save a backup copy", take
  the pen, then import that file.


IF THE FOLDER IS EVER LOST OR SCRAMBLED

Everything you need is in backups\. Open the model, press
"Use a shared folder...", then Deals -> Import JSON and pick the most
recent good snapshot.

Your own browser also keeps a copy of the last state it saw, so a
missing folder does not mean missing data.


WHAT THIS SETUP CANNOT DO

Two people cannot edit different deals at the same time. The pen covers
the whole file, not one deal - the second person waits. If that becomes
a daily problem, the model needs a real database rather than a folder,
and that is a separate piece of work.
