Which pages feed the spread

A full audited pack is mostly notes, and a note's table looks a lot like the statement it belongs to — a receivables note carries the gross figure where the balance sheet carries the net. Left in, they compete with the statements themselves. Only the primary statements are used; correct any page the model has called wrong.

What this does

It spreads the accounts into one canonical layout, then computes the credit ratios off that spread and grades how bankable the company looks. The extractor is an assistant, not an oracle: every figure it lifts is shown with its source and can be overwritten, and nothing is analysed until you have looked at the spread.

Scanned PDFs cannot be read — there is no OCR here. Type those in on the Spread tab.

Periods flows are per period; balances are as at the period end

Months = 12 for a full year, 6 for a half-year interim, and so on. LTM columns are derived, not typed.
